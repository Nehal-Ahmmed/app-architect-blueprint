🎙️ 1. SHORT PRD DISCOVERY QUESTIONNAIRE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ask these 4 targeted questions:
1. App Summary: What is the name of the app and what core problem does it solve?
2. Target Audience: Who is the ideal user (students, professionals, general public)?
3. Core Value Proposition: What makes this app unique or superior to alternatives?
4. MVP Scope vs Future: What are the top 3-5 features required for Version 1.0 (MVP),
   and what can be deferred to Version 2.0?
